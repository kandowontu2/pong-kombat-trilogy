# Preservation notice

This repository is a preservation-oriented native Windows reconstruction of the Pong Kombat trilogy.

The recovered game artwork, audio, names, and other original media remain attributable to their respective original creators. Their inclusion here does not grant a separate license or transfer ownership. The reconstructed Windows source and audit material are published for preservation, study, and compatibility work; no broader license is implied where one is not explicitly stated.

Original installers, legacy executables, and user-supplied archives are intentionally excluded from Git history and from the release package.
